# Cross-Pollination: Coaching Concepts Across Ecosystem

Coaching, training timelines, health/PT, competition planning, and communication pipelines are now treated as cross-cutting concerns.

## Applied To
- JuniorHome (Swift app coaching features)
- JuniorSOL (on-chain training/competition tracking)
- JuniorDrive (robotics training progression + sim timelines)
- JuniorStock (performance analytics + training plans)
- General deployment (user/coach journeys)

Powered by JuniorAGI_SDK modules like TimelineEngine and CommunicationAgent.
